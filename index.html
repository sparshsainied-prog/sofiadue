Aquae Sulis, Roman Britain, 90 CE

The Apothecary’s Husband

Livia’s father is dying, and when he arranges her marriage to Fabius, the son of his closest friend, duty must come first. But her heart belongs to Fabius’s brother, Marcus, while Fabius himself is in love with his best friend.

On their wedding day, an anonymous curse demanding vengeance for the marriage appears on the temple wall, setting in motion a chain of events that will alter all their lives forever.

Livia is determined to honour her father’s legacy as an apothecary, but she must navigate the ambitions of those who would sacrifice happiness for power and status.

Sofia Due

Born in London, Sofia now divides her time between the capital and the north Cornish coast. She still works as a lawyer, and spent many years in human rights law with refugees and survivors of modern slavery, themes which crop up in her stories.

Her Saturday job on Portobello Road as a teenager made it into Ed & Lily. She writes about second chances because the people who didn’t get it all their own way the first time often have the best stories.

She is the granddaughter of a Polish refugee. Her babcia’s own book, The Unsettled Account, went on being borrowed from libraries for years, and in 2018 it helped a family the Dues thought they had lost find them again. Sofia tells the story here.
